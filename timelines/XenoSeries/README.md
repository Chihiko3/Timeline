# Xeno Series Timeline

This folder owns the Xeno creative-lineage timeline:

- `releases.js`: earliest worldwide release dates and platform history
- `editorial-reading.js`: core experience, major changes, and research guidance
- `design-logic.js`: system-level design explanations
- `series-impact.js`: concrete long-term effects within the series
- `external-impact-research.js`: independently sourced industry or cultural impact
- `plot-summaries.js`: plot summaries and narrative innovations
- `timeline-images.js`: GM-managed artwork records
- `assets/covers`: all artwork imported through the GM image manager

The timeline follows three successive IP families associated with Tetsuya
Takahashi and related core teams: Xenogears, Xenosaga, and Xenoblade. They are
presented as a creative lineage, not as one canonical universe.

Primary cards cover standalone games, canonically important story expansions,
and versions whose rewritten scenario or systems create a distinct work.
Reference books, adaptations, guest appearances, ordinary ports, and unrelated
Monolith Soft games remain outside the timeline.

Ports remain platform records. A substantial edition or story expansion receives
its own primary card when it adds a distinct campaign or materially reframes the
original release.

Shared layout and card styling are owned by `../../common`.

The primary accent is a deep Monado crimson (`#a1263d`): it references the
series' recurring red blade motif while remaining distinct from Pokemon's
brighter orange-red.
