# Final Fantasy Timeline

This folder owns all Final Fantasy timeline content:

- `final-fantasy-releases.js`: release chronology and platform history
- `insights.js`: series position, core experience, change, legacy and research prompts
- `editorial-reading.js`: planner-oriented core experience, concrete change and research direction for every release
- `series-impact.js`: explicit, per-release long-term series impact decisions; `null` hides unsupported claims
- `external-impact-research.js`: source-backed external-impact research; entries without sufficient evidence are explicitly marked rather than inferred
- `plot-summaries.js`: plot summaries and optional narrative-innovation notes
- `final-fantasy-covers.js` and `final-fantasy-logos.js`: imported artwork maps
- `timeline-images.js`: GM-managed card artwork records
- `assets/covers`: cover and logo files used by the timeline

Shared timeline layout and card styling are in `../../common`.
